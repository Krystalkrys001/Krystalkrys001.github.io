DataCo Supply Chain Analytics

Sat with 180,519 real supply chain orders and asked a question that almost got past me: how does a business run an average delivery delay of practically zero days, while more than half its orders technically arrive late. Built a 3-page Power BI dashboard covering sales and profitability, shipping and delivery performance, and customer and order health, catching a duplicate KPI measure, a misleading 50/50 chart, and a sum-versus-average error along the way.

Caught & CorrectedAverage delivery delay sat near zero days, but 54.83% of orders were technically late underneath that average. Calculated a strict, honestly defined On-Time Delivery Rate of 17.84%, rejecting a looser definition that would have inflated the figure to roughly 45%.

Discount Motors: Hybrid & EV Sales Analysis

Analysed 6,693 used Toyota listings to test marketing's assumption that hybrid demand was too small to chase. Found hybrids already hold 30.5% of sales, second only to Petrol, and built a diagnostic Power BI dashboard around the real, same-model price premium instead of a misleading aggregate figure.

Caught & CorrectedThe source report defined the price premium using median price, but its stated result of 35% only reproduces using mean price. Recalculating with the documented method gave 29%, not 35%, and the dashboard was built on the corrected number.

TomaLink: Reducing Post-Harvest Tomato Losses in Nigeria

Worked as one of six Data Analysts on a 30-person hackathon team building a platform to cut Nigeria's tomato post-harvest losses, currently 40 to 45% of the national harvest. Sourced and cleaned the 5,000-row pilot dataset, and validated every figure on the final dashboard against raw data before it reached a slide.

Research that shaped the productResearched seven existing agricultural platforms before proposing a direction. Twiga Foods in Kenya nearly collapsed trying to own its own farms, trucks, and warehouses, a direct case for why TomaLink connects existing stakeholders instead of owning the chain itself.

Spotify Streaming Data Analysis

Built a Power BI dashboard analyzing streaming performance across 500 artists, 23 genres, and 43 countries, totaling roughly 8 million streams. Broke down total and lead streams by artist, feature-stream contribution, and streaming patterns by country, genre, primary language, and artist type, separating headline performance from collaborative features rather than reporting one flattened number.

What the split revealedCollaborative and solo streams landed almost evenly, 50.61% versus 49.39%, and the United States and hip-hop led their respective country and genre breakdowns, with English carrying roughly 6.1 million of the total streams by primary language.
